1. Customer Terms & Booking Guidelines

HomeServe AI connects homeowners and residents in Islamabad (including Jinnah Garden, Naval Anchorage, Soan Gardens, PWD, and CDA sectors) with verified independent service technicians (electricians, plumbers, AC specialists, solar engineers, and general handymen).

Fair Scope & Pricing Agreement: Before work begins, the customer and technician must inspect the site and confirm the scope of work, labor charges, materials required, and any potential extra tasks. Published catalog rates represent standard labor estimates for normal conditions; complex piping, high-ceiling wiring, or severe structural faults may require mutually agreed custom estimates.

Pay-After-Inspection Guarantee (No Advance Deposit): Customers are never required to pay an advance deposit on the platform before work commences. Payment is made directly to the technician (via Cash, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or bank transfer) only after the work has been performed and inspected to your full satisfaction.

Cancellations: Customers may cancel a service request free of charge while it is in the "new", "unassigned", or "assigned" state before the technician arrives on site. For emergency or active job assistance, contact customer support at 0339 4021643.

7-Day Free Rework Warranty: Every standard repair completed through the platform is backed by our 7-Day Free Rework Warranty. If the exact same issue persists within 7 calendar days of completion, submit a warranty revisit claim through your tracking link or via WhatsApp for a priority re-inspection at zero additional labor cost.

2. Technician / Service Partner Terms

Onboarding & Vetting: Technician applicants must provide valid legal documentation including Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) front and back, photograph, skill background, contact details, and secure authentication PIN. Formal verification and background vetting by administration are required before an account is approved to view or accept customer requests.

Code of Conduct: Approved technicians agree to behave courteously, perform duties safely and lawfully, use genuine parts/materials, provide honest assessments, and maintain standard transparent rates without unapproved price hikes.

GPS Arrival & Photographic Evidence: To maintain work transparency and dispute protection, technicians must record GPS arrival at the customer location (with accuracy within 150 meters) and submit clear before-work and after-work photographs. Any proposed extra work or unexpected material costs must be submitted digitally and approved by the customer before being billed.

Commission & Settlement: Platform commission (standard 10%) is calculated transparently upon completed jobs and recorded in an immutable ledger. Technicians maintain a deposit wallet balance to receive priority dispatch routing.
